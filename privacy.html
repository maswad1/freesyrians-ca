Privacy Policy

FreeSyrians.ca respects the privacy of its visitors. This page explains the basic privacy practices of the website in its current form.

Information we collect

The current website does not ask visitors to create accounts or submit personal information through the site. If contact forms, subscriptions, event registrations, analytics, or other interactive services are added in the future, this policy will be updated to explain what information is collected and why.

Information you choose to share

If you contact Free Syrians directly or later choose to submit a story, event, correction, resource, photograph, or other material, information you provide may be used to respond to you and to review the material you submitted. We will not publish personal contact information without permission.

External links

FreeSyrians.ca may link to external websites and organizations. Their privacy practices are controlled by those third parties, not by Free Syrians.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as the website grows or new features are introduced. The current version will always be available from the Privacy link in the site footer.
