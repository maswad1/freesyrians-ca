WHO WE ARE

A platform to educate, advocate, and connect.

Free Syrians brings together information, stories, resources, and community connections for Syrians and friends of Syria around the world. We seek to deepen understanding, amplify Syrian voices, defend freedom and human dignity, and help people connect around a shared hope for Syria’s future.

01

Educate

Provide context, history, stories, and resources that help people understand Syria and the experiences of its people.

02

Advocate

Stand for the rights, freedom, dignity, and meaningful participation of Syrians at home and throughout the diaspora.

03

Connect

Bring together Syrians, communities, supporters, initiatives, and organizations that want to contribute to a better future.

OUR MISSION

Freedom, dignity, justice, and a better future for Syria.

Free Syrians is an independent platform dedicated to Syrians who believe in freedom, dignity, justice, and a better future for Syria.

Our mission is to preserve the voices, stories, history, and aspirations of Syrians while creating a space that connects people across generations and around the world.

We believe Syria belongs to all its people. A future Syria should respect human dignity, protect individual rights, value its diversity, and allow every Syrian to participate freely in shaping the country’s future.

Free Syrians aims to inform, document, remember, connect, and contribute to that future.

SYRIA

A country of deep history, diverse communities, and a future still being written.

Syria is more than the conflicts that have defined recent headlines. It is a country shaped by ancient civilizations, historic cities, rich traditions, many communities, and generations of Syrians whose lives and stories extend far beyond politics and war.

This section will grow into a living guide to Syria: its history, cities, culture, people, modern experience, displacement and diaspora, and the ideas shaping its future.

Our goal is to help Syrians and non-Syrians understand the country in its full depth — remembering what was lost, preserving what remains, and looking toward what can still be built.

STORIES

Every Syrian story is part of Syria’s story.

History is not only written through governments, battles, and major events. It also lives in families, neighborhoods, classrooms, workplaces, journeys, traditions, friendships, losses, and new beginnings.

Stories will become a home for personal memories, eyewitness accounts, family histories, photographs, reflections, and experiences from Syrians inside the country and throughout the diaspora.

By preserving individual voices, we can help future generations understand not only what happened to Syria, but how Syrians lived through it, remembered it, rebuilt their lives, and continued to carry Syria with them wherever they went.

EVENTS

Gatherings that connect Syrians, communities, and generations.

Events will highlight upcoming gatherings connected to Syria and Syrian communities around the world, including cultural celebrations, commemorations, public talks, exhibitions, community meetings, educational programs, and other meaningful occasions.

We want this section to make it easier for Syrians and friends of Syria to discover opportunities to meet, learn, remember, celebrate, and participate.

As the site grows, events can include dates, locations, registration details, photographs, and recaps of past gatherings.

RESOURCES

Useful places to learn, document, connect, and find support.

Resources will bring together carefully selected links and references related to Syria, Syrian history and culture, community organizations, documentation projects, educational material, humanitarian support, and diaspora services.

The goal is not to create a long directory of links, but a useful and trusted starting point. As this section grows, resources can be organized by topic so visitors can quickly find what is relevant to them.

We will review and expand these resources over time, including valuable material from the original FreeSyrians.ca website where it still serves visitors well.

CONTACT

Connect with Free Syrians.

Free Syrians welcomes messages from people who want to contribute a story, share an event, suggest a resource, provide a correction, collaborate on a project, or help strengthen this platform.

This section will become the central point for general inquiries and community contributions. We will add the final contact method and submission options once the site structure is complete.
