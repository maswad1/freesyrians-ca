Homs

حمص

Homs is one of Syria’s major cities, situated near the country’s geographic heart. For centuries it has connected regions, communities, trade routes, traditions, and generations of Syrians whose memories are deeply tied to the city.

A Crossroads in Syria

Homs has long occupied an important position between Damascus, northern Syria, the Mediterranean coast, and the interior. That location helped shape the city as a place of movement, exchange, commerce, and cultural connection.

Heritage and Neighborhoods

The city’s identity is found not only in monuments but also in its neighborhoods, places of worship, markets, homes, family traditions, local food, humor, music, and the everyday life remembered by generations of residents.

Modern Experience

Homs endured profound hardship, destruction, displacement, and loss during Syria’s modern conflict. Its recent history is inseparable from the experiences of residents who stayed, left, returned, rebuilt, or carried memories of the city elsewhere.

A Living City

Homs remains more than the events that damaged it. Its story continues through its people, communities, culture, education, businesses, traditions, and the many Syrians who still identify strongly with the city wherever they live.
