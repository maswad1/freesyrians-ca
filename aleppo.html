Aleppo

حلب

Aleppo is one of Syria’s great historic cities and for centuries has stood at the meeting point of trade routes, cultures, languages, crafts, faiths, and communities. Its old city, markets, neighborhoods, and monumental Citadel reflect a history stretching back thousands of years.

A Historic Crossroads

Aleppo developed at the intersection of important routes linking Mesopotamia, Anatolia, the Mediterranean, and the Syrian interior. Trade and exchange helped make the city a major commercial and cultural center for centuries.

The Old City and Citadel

The Ancient City of Aleppo is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Its Citadel, mosques, churches, khans, hammams, covered markets, courtyards, and dense residential quarters preserve layers of architecture and urban life from many historical periods.

Craft, Music, and Everyday Culture

Aleppo is known for traditions of commerce, craftsmanship, cuisine, soap-making, music, and poetry. Its cultural identity has been shaped by diverse communities and by the rhythms of family life, markets, neighborhoods, and places of worship.

Destruction and Rebuilding

War caused immense damage to Aleppo’s people and historic fabric, including parts of the Old City. Yet Aleppo’s story also includes preservation, return, repair, reconstruction, and the determination of residents and diaspora communities to protect the city’s memory and future.
